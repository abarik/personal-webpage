Statistics

The language of psychological research: how to describe data, and when we can draw reliable conclusions from it. I guide students from the mathematical foundations through parametric tests to multivariate methods, in R and jamovi, in Hungarian and in English.

  • Mathematical FoundationsBA

  • Psychological Statistics 1 (parametric methods)BA

    • Worked examples of statistical tests

  • Psychological Statistics 3 (multivariate statistics)BA

    • Multivariate statistics in jamovi and R

  • Statistics for Psychologists 3BAin English

Computing

The digital foundations of academic work: spreadsheets, reference management and the use of library resources. My aim is for students to use the computer confidently and efficiently throughout their studies.

  • Computing 1BA

    • Reference managers – video series
    • Reference managers – notes (PDF)

  • Computing and Library SkillsBA

  • Computer Science IBAin English

Artificial intelligence

How do today's AI systems work, and what do they mean for psychology? We survey the field from the basics of machine learning to large language models, with its possibilities and limitations.

  • Artificial IntelligenceMA

Social networks

The structure of relationships says a lot: who is connected to whom, who sits at the centre of a network, and how information spreads through it. The course introduces the concepts and methods of social network analysis through practical examples.

  • Social Network Analysis

    • Social network analysis – notes

Knowledge space theory

Knowledge space theory models how pieces of knowledge build on each other within a subject, and how this lets us determine precisely what someone knows. The PhD course works through practical applications of the method.

  • Knowledge Space Theory in PracticePhD

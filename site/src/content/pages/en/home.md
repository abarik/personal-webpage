I have been an assistant professor at the Department of Social and Work Psychology,
Institute of Psychology, Faculty of Humanities, University of Debrecen since 2005.
I trained as a software engineer and mathematician, and received my PhD from the
University of Debrecen in 2013. My research covers **statistical data analysis**,
**speech research** and **education**, in particular gamification and algorithmic thinking.
